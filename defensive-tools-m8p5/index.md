## Pepper Spray (OC Spray)

### Effectiveness

- Causes intense burning in eyes
- Temporary blindness
- Respiratory irritation
- Effects last 30-45 minutes

### Legal Considerations

- Legal in most states for self-defense
- Size/concentration limits may apply
- Age restrictions may apply
- Prohibited in some locations (federal buildings, etc.)

### Usage Tips

- Know wind direction
- Short bursts at eyes
- Create distance after use
- May affect you too
- Check expiration dates
- Practice with training spray

## Personal Alarms

### How They Help

- 120+ decibels
- Draws attention
- May deter attacker
- Summons help

### Best Practices

- Keep accessible
- Test batteries regularly
- Combine with other strategies
- Know how to activate quickly

## Improvised Defense

### Everyday Items

Items that can be used defensively:
- Keys (fist striker)
- Pen (pressure point)
- Belt (swing weight)
- Bag (shield or swing)
- Hot drink (throw)
- Chair (barrier)

### The Best Defense

- Awareness to avoid situations
- De-escalation skills
- Physical distance
- Escape routes

## Training Requirements

### For Any Tool

- Learn proper use
- Practice under stress
- Understand limitations
- Know legal implications
- Regular refresher training

### Where to Learn

- Local self-defense classes
- Community safety courses
- Law enforcement programs
- Certified instructors

## When to Use Force

### Legal Principles

**Generally, force is justified when:**
- Facing imminent threat
- Reasonable belief of harm
- Proportional to threat
- No reasonable alternative

**Force is NOT justified for:**
- Property protection alone (varies)
- After threat has passed
- Disproportionate response
- Retaliation
