# Legal & Insurance Readiness

> **Protected Before It Happens** - Having proper insurance and understanding your legal rights before disaster strikes makes recovery much smoother.

## Insurance Essentials

### Types of Coverage

| Type | Covers |
|------|--------|
| Homeowners | Structure, contents, liability |
| Renters | Contents, liability |
| Flood | Flood damage (separate policy) |
| Earthquake | Earthquake damage (separate) |
| Auto | Vehicle damage, liability |
| Umbrella | Additional liability coverage |

### Key Points to Know

**Homeowners/Renters:**
- Know your deductible
- Understand coverage limits
- Review annually
- Document belongings

**Flood Insurance:**
- Not included in standard policies
- Required in many flood zones
- 30-day waiting period typically
- Available through NFIP

### Home Inventory

**Create and maintain:**
- Photos/video of all rooms
- List of valuable items
- Serial numbers of electronics
- Receipts for major purchases
- Appraisals for jewelry/art
- Store copies off-site and digitally

## Filing Claims

### After Disaster

1. Contact insurance company immediately
2. Document all damage (photos, video)
3. Don't throw anything away until documented
4. Make temporary repairs to prevent further damage
5. Keep all receipts
6. Track all communications

### Tips for Success

- Take before and after photos
- Document all conversations
- Get multiple repair estimates
- Review settlement carefully
- Understand depreciation vs. replacement value
- Know your appeal rights

## Legal Documents

### Essential Documents

- Will
- Power of attorney
- Healthcare proxy
- Living will/advance directive
- Trust documents (if applicable)
- Property deeds
- Insurance policies

### Keep Updated

- Review annually
- Update after major life changes
- Store originals safely
- Keep copies in multiple locations
- Inform relevant parties

## Disaster Assistance

### FEMA Assistance

**Available when disaster declared:**
- Housing assistance
- Other needs assistance
- Disaster unemployment
- Crisis counseling

**How to apply:**
- DisasterAssistance.gov
- 1-800-621-3362
- Local disaster recovery center

### Small Business Administration

- Low-interest disaster loans
- Home/personal property loans
- Business loans
- Economic injury loans

### Other Resources

- Red Cross
- State assistance programs
- Local charities
- Religious organizations
- Community foundations

## Property Protection

### Before Disaster

- Understand local ordinances
- Know building codes
- Document property condition
- Maintain insurance coverage
- Review policy annually

### After Disaster

- Secure property
- Prevent further damage
- Don't make major repairs without insurance consultation
- Keep unauthorized people out
- Document everything
