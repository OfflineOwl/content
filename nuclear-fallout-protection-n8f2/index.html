Nuclear Fallout Protection

⚠️ CRITICAL - DO THIS FIRST

YOU HAVE MINUTES TO ACT. If you receive warning of a nuclear event, immediately execute these steps. Do not wait to gather belongings. Do not wait for others. Every second counts.

🚨 IMMEDIATE ACTIONS (First 10 Minutes)

🕐 0-10 min | Critical Window

1. GET INSIDE IMMEDIATELY

The single most important action. Any building is better than outside.

🛡️ BEST SHELTER

  • Underground (basement, subway)
  • Center of large concrete building
  • Middle floors of high-rise

⚠️ AVOID

  • Near windows
  • Exterior walls
  • Top floors
  • Vehicles (minimal protection)

Interior rooms and basements provide the best protection

2. SEAL THE SPACE

Close all windows Close all doors Turn off HVAC Close fireplace damper Seal vents with tape/wet cloth

3. IF YOU WERE OUTSIDE

Decontaminate immediately. Do this BEFORE joining others inside.

Step 1: Remove Clothing

Removes ~90% of contamination

⏱️ THE CRITICAL FIRST 24 HOURS

Most dangerous fallout period
FIRST 24 HOURS
Radiation decreases by ~90% after this period

Fallout radiation intensity follows a predictable decay pattern:

Time After Event Radiation Level
1 hour 100% (most dangerous)
7 hours ~10%
24 hours ~1%
2 weeks ~0.1%

The "7-10 Rule": For every 7-fold increase in time, radiation decreases by 10x.

🏠 Shelter Protection Factors

Different materials provide different levels of protection. The Protection Factor (PF) indicates how much radiation is reduced.

Open air 1x protection
Wood frame house 2x protection
Brick house 10x protection
Concrete building 40x protection
Basement (3ft earth) 200x protection
Underground shelter 1000x protection

💧 Water & Food Safety

💧 Water Guidelines

✓ Sealed containers (bottles, cans) = SAFE
✓ Municipal water (pipes underground) = Usually SAFE
✗ Open containers = CONTAMINATED
✗ Well water = TEST FIRST

Food Safety

Food Type Status
Sealed cans/packages ✅ Safe (wash exterior)
Refrigerator food ✅ Safe if sealed
Food in closed cabinets ⚠️ Wash containers
Open food ❌ Discard
Fresh produce (outdoors) ❌ Discard

📻 Information Sources

Monitor these for official guidance:

  1. Emergency Alert System (EAS) - TV/Radio
  2. NOAA Weather Radio - All hazards
  3. Local emergency management
  4. FEMA - Federal guidance

Do NOT leave shelter to seek information. Use radio. Leaving shelter increases exposure.

🚪 When to Leave Shelter

⚠️ CRITICAL

Do NOT leave until official all-clear OR at least 24 hours have passed and you must leave for critical supplies.

If You Must Leave Early

  1. Minimize time outside (minutes, not hours)
  2. Cover all skin
  3. Wear mask/cover nose and mouth
  4. Do NOT touch anything unnecessarily
  5. Decontaminate immediately upon return
  6. Do not bring outside items inside

📋 Quick Action Checklist

□ GET INSIDE (any building, NOW)
□ Go to interior room / basement
□ CLOSE all windows and doors
□ Turn OFF ventilation / HVAC
□ SEAL gaps (tape, wet towels)
□ DECONTAMINATE if exposed outside
□ MONITOR radio for updates
□ STAY INSIDE minimum 24 hours
□ RATION supplies if needed
