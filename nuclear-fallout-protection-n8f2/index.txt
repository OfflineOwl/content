NUCLEAR FALLOUT PROTECTION
==========================
CRITICAL SURVIVAL INFORMATION

!!! YOU HAVE MINUTES TO ACT !!!

If you receive warning of a nuclear event, immediately execute
these steps. Do not wait to gather belongings. Do not wait for
others. Every second counts.

============================================================
SECTION 1: IMMEDIATE ACTIONS (FIRST 10 MINUTES)
============================================================

STEP 1: GET INSIDE IMMEDIATELY
------------------------------
The single most important action. Any building > outside.

BEST SHELTER:
+ Underground (basement, subway)
+ Center of large concrete building
+ Middle floors of high-rise

AVOID:
- Near windows
- Exterior walls
- Top floors
- Vehicles (minimal protection)

STEP 2: SEAL THE SPACE
----------------------
[ ] Close ALL windows
[ ] Close ALL doors
[ ] Turn OFF HVAC/ventilation
[ ] Close fireplace damper
[ ] Seal vents with tape or wet cloth

STEP 3: IF YOU WERE OUTSIDE - DECONTAMINATE
-------------------------------------------
Do this BEFORE joining others inside:

1. REMOVE clothing (removes ~90% of contamination)
2. BAG clothing in plastic, seal, set aside
3. SHOWER with soap and water, head to toe
4. SHAMPOO hair - NO CONDITIONER (binds particles)
5. PUT ON clean, uncontaminated clothes

============================================================
SECTION 2: THE CRITICAL FIRST 24 HOURS
============================================================

Radiation decreases significantly over time:

Time After Event    | Radiation Level
--------------------|----------------
1 hour              | 100% (MOST DANGEROUS)
7 hours             | ~10%
24 hours            | ~1%
2 weeks             | ~0.1%

THE "7-10 RULE":
For every 7x increase in time, radiation decreases 10x.

STAY SHELTERED MINIMUM 24 HOURS unless official guidance
says otherwise.

============================================================
SECTION 3: SHELTER PROTECTION FACTORS
============================================================

Different materials reduce radiation by different amounts:

Material                    | Protection Factor
----------------------------|------------------
Open air                    | 1x (no protection)
Wood frame house            | 2x
Brick house                 | 10x
Concrete building           | 40x
Basement (3ft earth cover)  | 200x
Underground shelter         | 1000x

Higher = Better. Get as much mass between you and outside.

============================================================
SECTION 4: WATER & FOOD SAFETY
============================================================

WATER:
+ Sealed containers (bottles, cans) = SAFE
+ Municipal water (pipes underground) = Usually SAFE
- Open containers = CONTAMINATED
- Well water = TEST FIRST before using

FOOD:
+ Sealed cans/packages = Safe (wash exterior first)
+ Refrigerator food = Safe if sealed
~ Closed cabinet food = Wash containers first
- Open food = DISCARD
- Fresh outdoor produce = DISCARD

============================================================
SECTION 5: INFORMATION SOURCES
============================================================

Monitor these for official guidance:
1. Emergency Alert System (EAS) - TV/Radio
2. NOAA Weather Radio - All hazards
3. Local emergency management
4. FEMA - Federal guidance

DO NOT leave shelter to seek information.
Use radio. Leaving increases exposure.

============================================================
SECTION 6: WHEN TO LEAVE SHELTER
============================================================

DO NOT leave until:
- Official all-clear given, OR
- Minimum 24 hours passed AND critical need

IF YOU MUST LEAVE EARLY:
1. Minimize time outside (minutes only)
2. Cover ALL skin
3. Wear mask/cover nose and mouth
4. Do NOT touch anything unnecessarily
5. Decontaminate immediately upon return
6. Do NOT bring outside items inside
