# Go-Bag Essentials by Family Member

> A one-size-fits-all go-bag doesn't exist. Each family member has different needs, capabilities, and roles. This guide helps you build customized go-bags for everyone.

## Go-Bag Philosophy

A go-bag (bug-out bag, BOB, 72-hour kit) should:
- Sustain you for 72+ hours
- Be light enough to carry
- Contain items you actually know how to use
- Be customized to the individual
- Be ready to grab and go

**Critical Rule:** A bag that's too heavy won't get carried. Be ruthless about weight.

---

## Weight Guidelines by Person

| Person | Max Bag Weight | Notes |
|--------|---------------|-------|
| Adult (fit) | 15-25% body weight | 25-40 lbs typical |
| Adult (average) | 10-15% body weight | 15-25 lbs |
| Elderly | 5-10% body weight | Focus on essentials |
| Teen (13-17) | 10-15% body weight | Build responsibility |
| Child (8-12) | 5-10% body weight | Keep it simple |
| Child (under 8) | Minimal | Comfort items only |

## Bag Selection Guide

### Features to Look For
- Multiple compartments
- Padded shoulder straps
- Waist belt (for heavier bags)
- Water-resistant material
- MOLLE webbing (optional)
- Durable zippers
- Neutral colors

### Bag Types

| Type | Pros | Cons | Best For |
|------|------|------|----------|
| **Backpack** | Hands-free, balanced | Can be heavy | Adults, teens |
| **Duffel** | Easy packing | Hard to carry far | Vehicle storage |
| **Rolling** | Easy transport | Fails on terrain | Elderly, airports |
| **Sling bag** | Quick access | Unbalanced | Secondary bag |

### Avoid
- Tactical/military appearance (draws attention)
- Bright colors (unless for visibility)
- Cheap zippers/straps
- Excessive compartments (hard to find things)

## Storage & Maintenance

### Storage Location
- Near primary exit
- Not in extreme heat/cold
- Accessible to all adults
- Away from pests

### Maintenance Schedule

**Monthly:**
- Check batteries
- Rotate water
- Check flashlights work

**Quarterly:**
- Check food expiration
- Rotate medications
- Review contents
- Adjust for season

**Annually:**
- Full inventory
- Replace expired items
- Update documents
- Adjust for family changes
