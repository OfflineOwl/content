# What NOT to Do During a CBR Event

> **Avoid Fatal Mistakes** - During chemical, biological, or radiological emergencies, panic leads to bad decisions. Knowing what NOT to do is as important as knowing what to do.

## General Mistakes to Avoid

### Don't Panic

- Panic spreads faster than threats
- Clear thinking saves lives
- Follow your plan
- Help others stay calm

### Don't Ignore Warnings

- Take all warnings seriously
- Don't wait to verify independently
- Seconds matter for chemical threats
- False alarm is better than missed warning

### Don't Crowd Emergency Services

- Don't call 911 for information
- Don't go to hospitals unless injured/symptomatic
- Keep lines clear for real emergencies
- Use designated information sources

## Chemical Event Mistakes

### Don't Go Outside to Look

- Chemical clouds are often invisible
- "Just checking" exposes you
- Stay inside until all-clear

### Don't Drive Through Affected Areas

- Vehicles are not sealed
- You may get stuck
- You spread contamination

### Don't Use Fans to "Clear the Air"

- This draws contaminated air in
- Turn off all ventilation
- Seal, don't ventilate

### Don't Mix Cleaning Chemicals

- Never mix bleach with ammonia
- Can create toxic gases
- Follow product instructions

## Biological Event Mistakes

### Don't Touch Others Unnecessarily

- Biological threats spread through contact
- Maintain distance
- Isolation protects everyone

### Don't Share Food, Drinks, or Items

- Cross-contamination risk
- Keep personal items personal
- Don't share respirators or masks

### Don't Ignore Quarantine Orders

- Quarantine protects everyone
- Breaking quarantine spreads disease
- Legal consequences may apply

### Don't Stockpile Antibiotics

- May not work on actual threat
- Misuse creates resistance
- Follow medical guidance

## Radiological Event Mistakes

### Don't Go Outside to Watch

- Curiosity can be fatal
- Radioactive fallout is invisible
- Stay inside for protection

### Don't Take Potassium Iodide Without Direction

- Only protects thyroid from one type of radiation
- May have side effects
- Only useful if radioactive iodine is present
- Follow official guidance

### Don't Use Conditioner After Exposure

- Conditioner binds radioactive particles to hair
- Use only shampoo
- Rinse thoroughly

### Don't Drink Alcohol "For Radiation"

- This is a myth
- Doesn't protect you
- Impairs judgment

## Information Mistakes

### Don't Spread Unverified Information

- Rumors cause panic
- Share only official information
- Check sources before sharing

### Don't Turn Off Your Phone

- Keep it charged
- Emergency alerts come via phone
- Communication is essential

### Don't Assume Authorities Will Notify You

- Sirens may not reach you
- Internet/power may be down
- Have multiple information sources

## Protection Mistakes

### Don't Use Plastic Bags as Masks

- Suffocation risk
- Not effective
- Use proper masks or wet cloth

### Don't Seal Room Too Tightly for Too Long

- You need air
- 2-3 hours maximum in sealed room
- Monitor for breathing difficulty

### Don't Leave During Event

- Exposure increases outside
- Wait for all-clear
- Shelter provides protection

## Quick Reference

```
DON'T DO THIS:

GENERAL:
- Panic
- Ignore warnings
- Crowd 911/hospitals
- Spread rumors

CHEMICAL:
- Go outside to look
- Drive through area
- Use fans/ventilation

BIOLOGICAL:
- Touch others
- Share items
- Break quarantine
- Self-medicate

RADIOLOGICAL:
- Go outside
- Take KI without direction
- Use conditioner
- Drink alcohol

ALWAYS:
- Stay inside
- Follow official guidance
- Wait for all-clear
```
