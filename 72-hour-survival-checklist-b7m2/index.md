# 72-Hour Survival Checklist (Urban & Rural)

> The first 72 hours after a disaster are critical. Help may not arrive immediately. This checklist ensures you have what you need to survive independently for at least three days.

## Why 72 Hours?

Emergency services recommend preparing for 72 hours because:
- First responders prioritize life-threatening situations
- Infrastructure damage may block access
- Supply chains take time to restore
- Government resources require mobilization

**Reality check:** Major disasters (hurricanes, earthquakes) often require 1-2 weeks of self-sufficiency. Treat 72 hours as a minimum.

---

## Water Checklist

**Minimum: 1 gallon per person per day (3 gallons per person for 72 hours)**

### Essential

- [ ] Bottled water (rotate every 6-12 months)
- [ ] Water purification tablets
- [ ] Portable water filter (LifeStraw, Sawyer, etc.)

### Urban Additions

- [ ] Know location of water heater (emergency water source)
- [ ] Know how to shut off main water valve
- [ ] Water BOB or bathtub liner for advance warning situations

### Rural Additions

- [ ] Map of nearby water sources (streams, ponds, wells)
- [ ] Extra purification supplies
- [ ] Bucket for water collection
- [ ] Solar still materials

---

## Food Checklist

**Minimum: 2,000 calories per person per day**

### Essential (No-Cook Options)

- [ ] Canned goods with pull-tops (beans, tuna, fruit, vegetables)
- [ ] Peanut butter or nut butter
- [ ] Crackers (rotate every 6 months)
- [ ] Dried fruit and nuts
- [ ] Energy bars or granola bars
- [ ] Comfort foods (candy, chocolate)
- [ ] Infant formula/baby food (if applicable)
- [ ] Pet food (if applicable)

### Extended Cooking Capability

- [ ] Camp stove with fuel
- [ ] Freeze-dried meals
- [ ] Instant rice/oatmeal
- [ ] Powdered milk
- [ ] Manual can opener (essential!)

### Urban Considerations

- [ ] Smaller quantities (may need to evacuate)
- [ ] Ready-to-eat options prioritized
- [ ] Non-cooking options for apartment dwellers

### Rural Considerations

- [ ] Larger stockpile feasible
- [ ] Outdoor cooking equipment
- [ ] Root cellar or cool storage options

## Shelter & Warmth Checklist

### Essential

- [ ] Emergency blankets (mylar, at least 2 per person)
- [ ] Sleeping bag or warm blankets
- [ ] Tarp (multiple uses)
- [ ] Duct tape
- [ ] Plastic sheeting (for sealing rooms)
- [ ] Extra clothing (seasonal appropriate)
- [ ] Rain gear

### Urban Additions

- [ ] Window coverings for insulation
- [ ] Draft blockers
- [ ] Indoor-safe heat source (if any exist for your situation)

### Rural Additions

- [ ] Tent or alternative shelter
- [ ] Firewood supply (seasoned)
- [ ] Axe or hatchet
- [ ] Fire starters (multiple types)
- [ ] Extra tarps

## Light & Power Checklist

### Essential

- [ ] Flashlights (LED, at least 2)
- [ ] Headlamp (hands-free essential)
- [ ] Extra batteries (for all devices)
- [ ] Battery-powered or hand-crank radio (AM/FM + NOAA)
- [ ] Candles (with safe holders)
- [ ] Matches and lighters (waterproof)

### Power Options

- [ ] Power bank for phones (fully charged)
- [ ] Solar charger
- [ ] Car phone charger
- [ ] Battery-powered lantern

### Urban Considerations

- [ ] Know breaker box location
- [ ] Surge protector for when power returns
- [ ] Battery-powered phone charger prioritized

### Rural Considerations

- [ ] Generator with fuel (if available)
- [ ] Extra fuel storage
- [ ] Solar panels (if established)
- [ ] Oil lamps with fuel

## First Aid & Medical Checklist

### Essential First Aid Kit

- [ ] Adhesive bandages (various sizes)
- [ ] Sterile gauze pads and rolls
- [ ] Medical tape
- [ ] Elastic bandages (ACE wraps)
- [ ] Triangular bandage (sling)
- [ ] Scissors (trauma shears preferred)
- [ ] Tweezers
- [ ] Disposable gloves (nitrile)
- [ ] Antiseptic wipes
- [ ] Antibiotic ointment
- [ ] Burn gel
- [ ] Instant cold packs
- [ ] Thermometer
- [ ] CPR mask

### Medications

- [ ] Pain relievers (acetaminophen, ibuprofen)
- [ ] Antihistamine (Benadryl)
- [ ] Anti-diarrheal
- [ ] Antacid
- [ ] Electrolyte packets (oral rehydration)
- [ ] Personal prescriptions (7-day supply minimum)
- [ ] Glasses/contacts (backup pair)

### Advanced Items

- [ ] Tourniquet (and training to use it)
- [ ] Hemostatic gauze
- [ ] SAM splint
- [ ] Chest seals (if trained)
