# Crowd Safety & Avoidance

> **The Danger of Crowds** - Large crowds pose unique risks. Knowing how to avoid, navigate, and escape crowds can prevent serious injury.

## Why Crowds Are Dangerous

### Crowd Dangers

- Crushing and trampling
- Surge events
- Limited movement
- Panic spread
- Violence can erupt
- Limited escape routes

### Crowd Crush vs. Stampede

**Crowd crush:** People compressed so tightly they can't move or breathe
**Stampede:** Panic-driven running (often follows crush fear)

## Crowd Avoidance

### Planning

- Know event schedules and busy times
- Plan trips during off-peak hours
- Use alternate routes
- Monitor local events
- Avoid protest areas

### Situational Awareness

**Warning signs of dangerous crowd:**
- Density where you can't move freely
- Pushing or jostling
- Inability to raise arms
- Difficulty breathing
- Anxiety/panic in crowd
- Blocked exits

## If in a Crowd

### Positioning

- Stay on edges, not center
- Near exits or escape routes
- Away from barriers/walls
- Avoid bottlenecks

### Movement

- Move with crowd flow
- Diagonal movement toward edges
- Don't fight the flow directly
- Steady, purposeful progress

### Protecting Yourself

- Arms in front of chest (boxer stance)
- Elbows out to create space
- Feet wide, stay upright
- If pushed, lean into crowd

## If Crowd Becomes Dangerous

### Immediate Actions

1. Don't panic
2. Create space around chest
3. Move toward edges
4. Look for exits
5. Stay upright

### If You Fall

1. Try to get up immediately
2. If you can't: Curl into ball
3. Protect head with arms
4. Face away from movement

### If Others Fall

- Help if safe
- Shield them
- Create space
- Don't become a victim too

## Crowd Surge Response

### During Surge

- Don't fight it
- Arms up, protect chest
- Move diagonally
- Stay on feet
- Work toward edges

### Post-Surge

- Continue moving to safety
- Check for injuries
- Help others if safe
- Report to authorities

## Special Situations

### Protests/Civil Unrest

- Avoid if possible
- Stay on periphery
- Don't engage
- Have exit strategy
- Leave at first sign of trouble

### Stadium/Concert Events

- Arrive early, know exits
- Stay near aisles
- Watch for warning signs
- Leave before main rush

### Panic Situations

- Stay calm
- Don't shout "fire" etc.
- Lead by example
- Move steadily, don't run
