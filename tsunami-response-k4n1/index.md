# Tsunami Response Basics

> **Minutes Matter** - A tsunami can arrive within minutes of an earthquake. Knowing how to respond can mean the difference between life and death in coastal areas.

## Warning Signs

### Natural Warning

**If you experience:**
- Strong earthquake lasting 20+ seconds
- Unusual ocean behavior (rapid recession, roaring sound)
- Strong ground shaking near coast

**Immediately move to high ground. Do not wait for official warning.**

### Official Warnings

- **Watch:** Distant event, more info coming
- **Advisory:** Minor waves expected
- **Warning:** Dangerous waves expected, evacuate
- **All Clear:** Danger has passed

## Immediate Response

### If Earthquake Near Coast

1. Drop, cover, hold on (earthquake)
2. When shaking stops, move immediately
3. Go inland or to high ground
4. Don't wait for official warning
5. Move to 100+ feet elevation or 2+ miles inland

### If You See Ocean Receding

- This is a major warning sign
- Tsunami may arrive in minutes
- Move immediately to high ground
- Don't go to beach to watch

### If Official Warning Issued

- Evacuate immediately
- Follow designated evacuation routes
- Move inland or uphill
- Don't return until all-clear given

## Evacuation Guidelines

### Where to Go

- 100+ feet above sea level
- 2+ miles inland
- Upper floors of reinforced concrete buildings (last resort)
- Designated tsunami evacuation sites

### What NOT to Do

- Don't stay to watch
- Don't go to beach
- Don't drive if you can walk to safety
- Don't return early
- Don't assume one wave is all

### Multiple Waves

- First wave may not be largest
- Waves can continue for hours
- Wait for official all-clear
- Waves arrive 10-60 minutes apart

## If Trapped by Tsunami

### In Water

- Grab floating debris
- Don't try to swim against current
- Get to high ground when possible

### In Building

- Move to highest floor
- Move to ocean-side of building
- Grab something sturdy
- Expect debris in water

## After Tsunami

### Immediate Safety

- Stay away from coast until all-clear
- Avoid flood water (contaminated, debris)
- Watch for downed power lines
- Stay out of damaged buildings

### Returning Home

- Only when authorities say safe
- Expect damage and debris
- Watch for secondary hazards
- Document damage for insurance
