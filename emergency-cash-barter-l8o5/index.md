# Emergency Cash & Barter Basics

> **When Cards Don't Work** - During emergencies, electronic payment systems may fail. Cash and barter become essential for obtaining goods and services.

## Why Cash Matters in Emergencies

### Systems That Can Fail

- Credit/debit card networks
- ATMs (no power, no network)
- Bank systems
- Internet-based payments
- Point-of-sale systems

### When Cash is Critical

- Power outages
- Natural disasters
- Bank holidays/closures
- Network outages
- Civil unrest
- Economic disruptions

---

## Emergency Cash Guidelines

### How Much to Keep

| Duration | Recommended |
|----------|-------------|
| 72-hour kit | $200-300 |
| 2-week supply | $500-1000 |
| Extended prep | $1000+ |

### Denomination Strategy

**Keep variety of bills:**
- $100s (high-value, compact)
- $50s (moderate purchases)
- $20s (most useful)
- $10s (smaller transactions)
- $5s (making change)
- $1s (small purchases)
- Quarters (vending, laundry)

**Recommended mix:**
- 40% in $20s
- 30% in smaller bills ($10, $5, $1)
- 30% in larger bills ($50, $100)
- $10-20 in quarters

### Storage

**Requirements:**
- Waterproof container
- Fireproof if possible
- Accessible but secure
- Not all in one location
- Some in go-bag

**Locations:**
- Home safe
- Hidden at home
- Go-bag
- Vehicle (secured)
- With trusted family member

## Barter Basics

### What is Barter?

Direct exchange of goods/services without money.

### High-Value Barter Items

**Consumables:**
| Item | Why Valuable |
|------|-------------|
| Water/filters | Essential, hard to get |
| Food (shelf-stable) | Universal need |
| Fuel | Scarce during emergencies |
| Medications | Critical needs |
| Batteries | Power everything |
| Alcohol (small bottles) | Multiple uses |
| Coffee | Comfort item |
| Tobacco | Addictive, wanted |

**Tools/Supplies:**
| Item | Why Valuable |
|------|-------------|
| Manual can opener | Essential, often forgotten |
| Fire starters | Basic need |
| Hygiene items | Sanitation |
| First aid supplies | Medical needs |
| Duct tape | Universal usefulness |
| Rope/cordage | Many applications |

**Skills as barter:**
- Medical knowledge
- Mechanical repair
- Electrical skills
- Construction/carpentry
- Food preservation
- Security skills

### Barter Tips

**When bartering:**
- Know item's value
- Don't reveal full inventory
- Start high in negotiations
- Have multiple items to trade
- Build relationships first
- Fair dealing builds trust

**Safety:**
- Meet in neutral locations
- Bring backup if possible
- Don't reveal where you live
- Don't show all you have
- Trust your instincts

---

## Protecting Your Resources

### Operational Security

- Don't advertise what you have
- Vary where you get supplies
- Don't make yourself a target
- Share wisely
- Maintain discretion

### Documentation

- Know what you have
- Keep records hidden
- Have inventory for insurance
