# Offline Maps & Navigation

> **When GPS Fails** - Digital navigation depends on connectivity. Offline maps and traditional navigation skills ensure you can find your way when technology fails.

## Why Offline Navigation Matters

### When GPS Might Fail

- No cell service (rural, disaster)
- Phone battery dead
- Satellites unavailable
- Damaged infrastructure
- Jamming/interference

## Offline Digital Maps

### Downloading Maps

**Google Maps:**
1. Open Google Maps
2. Tap profile picture
3. Select "Offline maps"
4. Choose area to download
5. Maps expire after 30 days (update)

**Apple Maps:**
1. Search for location
2. Tap route
3. Select "Download" for offline use

**Dedicated Apps:**
- OsmAnd (OpenStreetMap based)
- Maps.me
- Gaia GPS
- Avenza Maps

### Tips for Offline Maps

- Download home area + evacuation routes
- Include wide buffer zone
- Update before known events
- Include multiple zoom levels
- Note download date

## Paper Maps

### What to Have

- State highway map
- Local street atlas
- Topographic maps (for rural areas)
- Regional map showing major routes
- Maps of evacuation routes

### Map Care

- Keep in waterproof container
- Store in vehicle and go-bag
- Replace when worn or outdated
- Laminate frequently used maps

## Basic Map Reading

### Map Elements

- **Title:** What area is covered
- **Scale:** Distance relationship
- **Legend:** Symbol meanings
- **North arrow:** Orientation
- **Contour lines:** Elevation (topo maps)

### Scale Understanding

| Scale | 1 inch = |
|-------|----------|
| 1:24,000 | 2,000 feet |
| 1:62,500 | ~1 mile |
| 1:100,000 | ~1.6 miles |
| 1:250,000 | ~4 miles |

### Finding Your Location

1. Identify two or more known landmarks
2. Match landmarks to map symbols
3. Use triangulation if needed
4. Verify with other features

## Compass Basics

### How a Compass Works

- Magnetic needle points to magnetic north
- Adjust for declination (difference from true north)
- Used with map for navigation

### Basic Compass Use

1. Hold compass level
2. Let needle settle
3. Rotate housing to align needle with N
4. Read bearing from direction of travel arrow

### Declination

Magnetic north ≠ True north
- Varies by location
- Maps show local declination
- Adjust compass or calculation

## Navigation Without Tools

### Natural Navigation

**Finding North:**
- Sun rises east, sets west
- At noon, sun is south (northern hemisphere)
- North Star (Polaris) indicates north at night
- Moss tends to grow on north side of trees (unreliable)

**Using Shadows:**
1. Place stick in ground
2. Mark shadow tip
3. Wait 15 minutes
4. Mark new shadow tip
5. Line between marks = East-West

### Staying Found

- Always know where you came from
- Mark your path
- Use landmarks
- Keep track of turns
- Note time and distance traveled
