# Extreme Heat Survival

> **Heat Kills Silently** - Extreme heat causes more deaths than any other weather event. Understanding heat illness and how to stay cool without AC can save lives.

## Understanding Heat Danger

### Heat Index

Combines temperature and humidity to show how hot it feels.

| Heat Index | Risk Level |
|------------|------------|
| 80-90°F | Caution |
| 91-103°F | Extreme Caution |
| 103-124°F | Danger |
| 125°F+ | Extreme Danger |

### Who's at Risk

- Elderly (65+)
- Infants and young children
- People with chronic conditions
- Those without AC
- Outdoor workers
- Athletes

## Heat Illness Progression

### Heat Cramps
**Early warning**
- Muscle cramps
- Heavy sweating

### Heat Exhaustion
**Serious**
- Heavy sweating
- Cold, pale, clammy skin
- Weak pulse
- Nausea, vomiting
- Weakness, fatigue

### Heat Stroke
**EMERGENCY**
- High body temp (103°F+)
- Hot, red, dry skin
- Rapid pulse
- Confusion
- Loss of consciousness

## Staying Cool Without AC

### Body Cooling
- Wet cloths on pulse points (wrists, neck, ankles)
- Cold water on skin
- Misting with spray bottle
- Cool (not cold) shower
- Wet hair and let evaporate

### Environment
- Close blinds/curtains
- Use fans
- Open windows at night
- Stay on lowest floor
- Create cross-ventilation
- Block direct sunlight

### Hydration
- Drink before you're thirsty
- Water is best
- Avoid alcohol and caffeine
- Include electrolytes if sweating heavily
- Monitor urine color (pale = hydrated)

### Activity
- Stay indoors during hottest hours (10am-4pm)
- Minimize physical activity
- Rest frequently
- Do necessary tasks early morning

### Clothing
- Light-colored, loose-fitting
- Natural fibers (cotton, linen)
- Minimal coverage when indoors
- Hat and sunglasses outdoors

## Emergency Response

### Heat Exhaustion Treatment
1. Move to cool area
2. Lie down, elevate legs
3. Remove excess clothing
4. Apply cool, wet cloths
5. Sip water slowly
6. Seek medical care if worsening

### Heat Stroke Treatment (EMERGENCY)
1. Call 911
2. Move to cool area
3. Cool body rapidly
4. Cold water immersion if possible
5. Ice packs to armpits, neck, groin
6. Do NOT give fluids if unconscious
