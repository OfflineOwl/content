Understanding Calorie Needs

  • Sedentary: Little or no exercise, desk work
  • Moderate: Light exercise, some walking
  • Active: Physical work, significant activity

Rationing Guidelines

  • 80%: Comfortable for short-term, some hunger
  • 60%: Noticeable hunger, energy decrease
  • 50%: Significant discomfort, reduced activity
  • 40%: Survival only, not sustainable long-term

Calorie Estimates for Common Foods

  • Rice (dry, 1 lb): ~1,600 calories
  • Dried beans (1 lb): ~1,500 calories
  • Peanut butter (16 oz jar): ~2,700 calories
  • Canned tuna (5 oz): ~100-150 calories
  • Canned soup (can): ~200-300 calories
  • Granola bar: ~100-200 calories
