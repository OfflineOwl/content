💧 Improvised Water Filters

Visual guide to building emergency water filtration systems

⚠️

Critical Warning

Improvised filters remove particles and improve taste but do not make water safe to drink. Always disinfect filtered water through boiling, chemical treatment, or UV before drinking.

Basic Sand/Charcoal Filter

The most effective improvised filter uses layers of natural materials. Each layer serves a specific purpose in cleaning the water.

✓ Filtered Water Out

Cloth/Grass (Top)

Prevents sand from floating up. Use clean cotton, grass, or coffee filters.

Fine Sand (2-3 inches)

Primary filtration layer. Removes small particles and some bacteria. Use cleanest sand available.

Crushed Charcoal (2-3 inches)

Absorbs chemicals, removes odors, improves taste. Use hardwood charcoal, NOT briquettes.

Gravel (1-2 inches)

Supports charcoal layer, prevents clogging. Pea-sized stones work best.

Rocks/Pebbles (2-3 inches)

Drainage layer. Allows water to exit without carrying filter materials.

Materials You Can Use

Container Options

  • 2-liter plastic bottle (cut bottom off)
  • 5-gallon bucket with holes
  • Large PVC pipe
  • Bamboo section
  • Birch bark cone
  • Clean trash can

Charcoal Sources

  • Hardwood fire (cooled completely)
  • Aquarium charcoal
  • Activated charcoal
  • NOT charcoal briquettes (contain additives)

Sand/Gravel Sources

  • Stream beds
  • Lake shores
  • Construction sites
  • Hardware stores
  • Aquarium gravel

Building Instructions

Prepare Container

Cut bottom off plastic bottle, or drill holes in bottom of bucket. Create a way for water to exit while keeping materials in.

Add Drainage Layer

Place 2-3 inches of rocks or large pebbles at the bottom (outlet end). This prevents clogging.

Add Gravel Layer

Add 1-2 inches of pea-sized gravel on top of rocks. This supports the charcoal layer.

Add Charcoal Layer

Crush charcoal into small pieces. Add 2-3 inches. This is the key filtration layer for chemicals and taste.

Add Sand Layer

Add 2-3 inches of fine sand. Use cleanest sand available. This is the primary particle filter.

Add Top Layer

Cover with cloth, grass, or coffee filter to prevent sand disturbance when adding water.

Prime the Filter

Run water through 2-3 times before using. First runs will be cloudy. Discard until water runs clearer.

Alternative Quick Filters

Cloth Filter

Simplest emergency method

  • Fold clean cloth 4+ times
  • Pour water through slowly
  • Removes large particles only
  • Must disinfect after

Coffee Filter

Better than cloth

  • Use multiple filters
  • Removes finer particles
  • Good for pre-filtering turbid water
  • Must disinfect after

Settling

Requires only time

  • Let water sit 30+ minutes
  • Particles settle to bottom
  • Carefully pour off top
  • Must disinfect after

Solar Still

Filters AND disinfects

  • Evaporation removes all particles
  • UV from sun disinfects
  • Slow but effective
  • See desalination guide

Maintenance Tips

Keep Your Filter Working

  • Replace charcoal every 50 gallons
  • Backflush if flow slows
  • Replace sand if odor persists
  • Clean container regularly
  • Pre-filter very dirty water
  • Store filter upside down when not in use
