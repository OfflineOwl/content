# Landslide & Mudflow Awareness

> **When the Ground Moves** - Landslides can occur with little warning. Knowing the risks and warning signs can save your life.

## Understanding Landslides

### Types

| Type | Speed | Cause |
|------|-------|-------|
| Rockfall | Very fast | Gravity, weathering |
| Debris flow | Fast | Heavy rain, saturation |
| Mudflow | Fast | Water + soil |
| Landslide | Variable | Slope failure |
| Creep | Very slow | Gradual movement |

### Triggers

- Heavy rainfall
- Rapid snowmelt
- Earthquakes
- Volcanic eruptions
- Human activity (construction, deforestation)
- Erosion

## Know Your Risk

### High-Risk Areas

- Steep slopes
- Previous slide areas
- Near drainage channels
- Burn scar areas (wildfires)
- Areas of recent construction
- Near cliffs or embankments

### Warning Signs

**Ground movement:**
- New cracks in ground
- Bulging ground
- Tilting trees or fences
- Doors/windows sticking
- Cracks in foundations

**Water patterns:**
- New springs or seeps
- Unusual water flow
- Saturated ground
- Rising water in streams

**Sounds:**
- Rumbling (getting louder)
- Cracking trees or rocks
- Unusual sounds from ground

## Before Potential Landslide

### If Warning Issued

- Monitor local news
- Be ready to evacuate
- Know evacuation routes
- Identify high ground nearby
- Don't wait to see it

### If Heavy Rain Expected

- In risk area: Consider leaving early
- Monitor conditions
- Stay alert for warning signs
- Have go-bag ready

## During Landslide

### If Indoors

- If time, evacuate to higher ground
- If no time: Move to second floor
- Curl into ball, protect head
- Stay away from path of flow

### If Outdoors

- Move away from path
- Move perpendicular to flow direction
- Get to high ground
- Avoid river valleys
- If caught: Curl into ball, protect head

### If in Vehicle

- Don't cross flooded areas
- Watch for debris flow across roads
- If trapped: Stay in vehicle unless debris approaching
- Move to high ground if possible

## After Landslide

### Immediate Safety

- Stay away from slide area
- More slides may occur
- Avoid downstream areas
- Report to authorities

### Assessment

- Don't enter damaged buildings
- Check for gas leaks
- Report damage
- Document with photos
