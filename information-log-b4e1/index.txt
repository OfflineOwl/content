EMERGENCY INFORMATION LOG
=========================

Maintaining a written log during emergencies helps track what's
happening, decisions made, and creates a record for later use.

WHY KEEP A LOG
--------------

DURING EMERGENCY:
- Track changing situation
- Remember what happened when
- Record decisions and reasons
- Track resources used
- Note contacts made

AFTER EMERGENCY:
- Insurance claims
- Legal documentation
- Lessons learned
- FEMA/assistance applications
- Personal record

WHAT TO LOG
-----------

BASIC ENTRIES:
Date: __________
Time: __________
What happened: _________________________________
Source of information: _________________________
Actions taken: _________________________________
Decisions made: ________________________________
Why: __________________________________________

SPECIFIC ITEMS TO RECORD:
- Official announcements
- Weather/conditions changes
- Family status updates
- Resource status
- Contacts with authorities
- Damage discovered
- Expenses incurred
- Photos taken (note numbers/files)

LOG FORMAT OPTIONS
------------------

SIMPLE NOTEBOOK:
Just write entries chronologically
Include date, time, event, source

STRUCTURED LOG:
Pre-printed forms
Consistent categories
Easy to reference

DIGITAL LOG:
Phone notes app
Photos with timestamps
Voice memos

MULTIPLE METHODS:
Backup in case one is lost
Paper + digital ideal

LOGGING PRACTICES
-----------------

TIMING:
- Log significant events as they happen
- Review and summarize daily
- Don't try to remember everything later

ACCURACY:
- Record exact times when possible
- Note sources of information
- Be factual, not emotional
- Record what you observed vs. heard

COMPLETENESS:
- Better to over-document
- Include "nothing happened" entries
- Note routine activities too
- Record resource usage

PRESERVATION:
- Keep log dry and safe
- Take photos of written log
- Store copies separately
- Don't lose your documentation

USING LOG FOR CLAIMS
--------------------

INSURANCE CLAIMS:
Document before cleanup:
- Photos of all damage
- List of damaged items
- Dates of damage discovered
- Temporary repair costs
- All related expenses

FEMA APPLICATIONS:
- Dates of disaster impact
- Actions taken
- Expenses incurred
- Damage timeline

LEGAL PURPOSES:
- Chronological record
- Factual observations
- Decision rationale
- Contacts and communications

DIGITAL LOGGING TIPS
--------------------

PHOTOS:
- Enable timestamps
- Back up immediately
- Note what each photo shows
- Capture context, not just damage

VOICE MEMOS:
- Speak date and time
- Describe situation
- Note location
- Summarize daily

APPS:
- Notes apps work
- Emergency apps may have log features
- Cloud backup when possible
- Don't rely solely on digital
