# Looting Awareness & Prevention

> **Protect What's Yours** - During disasters, looting can occur. Understanding the threat and taking precautions helps protect your property.

## Understanding the Threat

### When Looting Occurs

- Extended power outages
- Civil unrest
- After major disasters
- When police are overwhelmed
- Evacuation situations

### Targets

- Unoccupied homes (evacuation)
- Businesses
- Damaged properties
- Areas without law enforcement presence

## Prevention Strategies

### Make Home Look Occupied

Even when evacuated:
- Timer-controlled lights
- Radio on timer
- Vehicles in driveway (if safe)
- Mail/paper stopped
- Lawn maintained (if possible)

### Physical Security

- Strong doors and locks
- Secured windows
- Motion-activated lights
- Visible security cameras
- Security system signs

### Don't Advertise

- Don't post evacuation on social media
- Don't discuss plans publicly
- Don't leave valuable items visible
- Remove garage door openers from vehicles

## If You're Home

### Deterrence

- Presence is the best deterrent
- Visible activity
- Lights on
- Dogs (or dog sounds)
- Neighbors aware you're there

### Community Watch

- Coordinate with neighbors
- Share watch schedules
- Communication plan
- Know who's home

### If Confronted

- Personal safety over property
- Don't confront armed individuals
- Call police when safe
- Document if possible

## During Evacuation

### Before Leaving

- Secure all entry points
- Photograph valuables
- Hide or secure valuables
- Don't leave obvious targets
- Inform trusted neighbors

### What to Take

- Important documents
- Irreplaceable items
- Small valuables
- Medications

### What NOT to Do

- Don't announce evacuation
- Don't leave keys hidden
- Don't leave garage door openers
- Don't assume insurance covers everything

## Legal Considerations

### Know Your Rights

- Property rights vary by jurisdiction
- Self-defense laws vary
- Understand local laws
- Document all losses

### Insurance

- Document belongings before disaster
- Understand policy coverage
- File claims promptly
- Keep receipts
