# When Communication Silence is Critical

> **Sometimes Silence Saves** - In certain emergency situations, maintaining communication silence protects you. Know when to go quiet.

## When Silence Matters

### Active Threat Situations

- Home invasion
- Active shooter
- Hostile individuals nearby
- When hiding from danger

### Tactical Situations

- Evading pursuit
- Moving through dangerous areas
- When others might intercept communications

### Operational Security

- Don't reveal your location
- Don't reveal your resources
- Don't reveal your plans
- Don't reveal who's with you

## Radio/Communication Silence

### Why It Matters

- Signals can be heard by others
- Location can be determined
- Information can be gathered
- Draw attention to yourself

### When to Go Silent

- During active threats
- When ordered by authorities
- When hostiles might monitor
- When moving covertly

### Breaking Silence

Only break silence for:
- Immediate life-threatening emergency
- All-clear received
- Reaching safe location
- Coordinating critical action

## Sound Discipline

### In Hiding

- Silence phones completely
- Control breathing
- Minimize movement
- Control children/pets
- No unnecessary communication

### Movement

- Move quietly
- Avoid talking
- Use hand signals
- Minimize gear noise
- Careful footsteps

## Light Discipline

### Why Lights Matter

- Light travels far
- Reveals location
- Visible through windows
- Reflects and bounces

### Light Silence

- No flashlights
- No phone screens
- Cover windows
- No fires or flames
- Red light if necessary (less visible)

## Digital Silence

### When to Limit

- Location sharing active
- Suspicious monitoring
- Operational security needed

### What to Consider

- Phone tracking
- Social media posts
- Location tags
- Check-ins
- Photos with metadata

### Going Dark

- Airplane mode
- Location services off
- No social media
- No unnecessary calls
- No text locations

## Family Communication During Silence

### Pre-Arranged Signals

Establish before emergency:
- Visual signals only
- Hand signals
- Tap codes
- Written notes

### Maintain Contact

Even in silence:
- Stay together
- Visual check-ins
- Non-verbal cues
- Pre-established schedule

## Breaking Silence

### When Appropriate

- Immediate life threat (call 911)
- Emergency requiring help
- All-clear confirmed
- Reaching safe location
- Critical coordination needed

### How to Break

- Keep it brief
- Essential information only
- Return to silence after
- Move if possible after transmitting

## Quick Reference

```
GO SILENT WHEN:
- Active threat present
- Hiding from danger
- Moving through danger
- Ordered by authorities

SILENCE INCLUDES:
- No phone calls
- No radio transmission
- No lights
- No unnecessary sound
- No social media

BREAK SILENCE FOR:
- Life-threatening emergency
- Official all-clear
- Reaching safety
- Critical coordination

MAINTAIN DISCIPLINE:
- Phones silent/off
- Minimal movement
- Cover light sources
- Pre-arranged signals only
```
