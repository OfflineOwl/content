Decontamination Basics

⚠️ CRITICAL SAFETY

If you've been exposed to hazardous materials, proper decontamination reduces harm and prevents spreading contamination to others. Act quickly but carefully.

🎯 Identify Your Contamination Type

Different contamination types require different approaches:

Industrial spills, household chemicals, unknown substances

Steps for Chemical Decon:
1Remove ALL contaminated clothing immediately
2Rinse with large amounts of water (15+ minutes)
3Wash with soap and water thoroughly
4Flush eyes if affected (15-20 minutes)
5Seek medical attention immediately
⚠️ Warning: Do NOT use hot water - it can increase absorption through skin

Infectious disease, bodily fluids, biological agents

Steps for Biological Decon:
1Remove contaminated clothing carefully
2Shower with soap and warm water
3Wash hands for at least 20 seconds
4Clean under fingernails
5Monitor for symptoms over coming days
⚠️ Warning: Avoid touching face during decontamination

Nuclear fallout, radiation exposure, dirty bomb

Steps for Radiological Decon:
1Remove outer clothing (removes ~90% contamination)
2Bag clothing and set aside
3Shower with shampoo ONLY (no conditioner)
4Blow nose, wipe ears, clean eyelids gently
5Put on clean, uncontaminated clothes
⚠️ Warning: NEVER use conditioner - it binds radioactive particles to hair

✓ Do's and Don'ts

✓Use lukewarm water

✓Clean under fingernails
✓Bag contaminated items
✓Wear PPE when helping others

❌ DON'T

✗Use HOT water (opens pores)
✗Scrub vigorously (damages skin)
✗Use conditioner (binds particles)
✗Contaminate clean areas
✗Eat/drink until fully clean

📋 Quick Reference

DECONTAMINATION STEPS

1. REMOVE clothing → Removes ~90% of contamination
2. RINSE with water → Head to toe, not hot
3. WASH with soap → Hair with shampoo, no conditioner
4. DRY and dress → Clean clothes only

EYES: Flush 15-20 minutes, inner to outer corner
WOUNDS: Cover before washing, avoid contaminating
