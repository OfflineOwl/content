🧪 Method 4: DIY Filtration

Build your own filter from natural materials:

🔬 DIY Sand/Charcoal Filter

Cloth: Keeps sand in place
Sand: Fine filtration (2-3 inches)
Charcoal: Absorbs chemicals (2-3 inches)
Gravel: Keeps charcoal in (1-2 inches)
Rocks: Drainage layer (2-3 inches)
⚠️ Warning: DIY filters remove particles but NOT all pathogens. Always disinfect after filtering!

Remember: Filtration removes particles but most DIY filters do NOT remove all pathogens. Always disinfect after filtering!

🔄 Combining Methods (Maximum Safety)

For questionable water sources, combine methods:

  1. Pre-filter (cloth, coffee filter)
  2. Filter (commercial or DIY)
  3. Disinfect (boil, chemical, or UV)
  4. Store safely (covered, clean container)
