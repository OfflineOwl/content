Quarantine vs Isolation Explained

🛡️ IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

These terms are often confused but serve different purposes. Understanding the difference helps you respond appropriately during disease outbreaks and protect your family.

🏠 Setting Up for Isolation

The Sick Room

Isolation Room Setup

  • Dedicated room with door that closes
  • Own bathroom if possible
  • Good ventilation (open window if safe)
  • All supplies within the room
  • Single designated caregiver

🧠 Mental Health During Isolation

Isolation and quarantine are emotionally challenging:

Common Challenges

  • Loneliness and boredom
  • Anxiety about illness
  • Cabin fever
  • Disruption of routine
  • Financial stress

Coping Strategies

  • Video calls with family/friends
  • Books, movies, games
  • Light exercise if able
  • Maintain daily routines
  • Limit news consumption

📋 Quick Reference

ISOLATION = SICK
├── Confirmed illness
├── Stay separated until recovered  
├── Caregiver uses PPE
└── Dedicated room/bathroom

QUARANTINE = EXPOSED  
├── Was exposed, may not be sick yet
├── Monitor for symptoms daily
├── Stay home during incubation period
└── May still develop illness

BOTH REQUIRE:
├── Staying home
├── Separation from others
├── Monitoring health
└── Following official guidance

⚠️ When to Seek Medical Care

⚠️ Emergency Warning Signs

  • ❌ Difficulty breathing or shortness of breath
  • ❌ Persistent pain or pressure in chest
  • ❌ Confusion or inability to stay awake
  • ❌ Pale, gray, or blue-colored skin/lips
  • ❌ Severe or worsening symptoms

Call emergency services or go to emergency room immediately
