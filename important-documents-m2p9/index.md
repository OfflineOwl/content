# Important Documents Offline Kit

> **Proof When Systems Are Down** - When computers and records are inaccessible, having physical copies of critical documents can be the difference between chaos and clarity.

## Why Physical Documents Matter

During emergencies:
- Computer systems may be down
- Internet may be unavailable
- You may be away from home
- Offices may be closed
- Digital records may be inaccessible

## How to Store

### Physical Storage

**Document safe:**
- Fireproof, waterproof
- At home
- Contains originals of critical documents

**Go-bag copy:**
- Waterproof bag/container
- Copies only (not originals)
- Ready to grab

**Off-site copy:**
- Safe deposit box
- Trusted family member
- Another location you can access

### Digital Backup

**USB drive:**
- Encrypted
- In go-bag
- Updated annually

**Cloud storage:**
- Encrypted
- Accessible from anywhere
- Requires internet

**Email to self:**
- Encrypted documents
- Emergency access
- Accessible anywhere

## Document Protection

### Original Documents

Keep originals in:
- Fireproof safe at home
- Safe deposit box
- Secure, accessible location

### Copies

- Clearly mark as "COPY"
- Laminate important copies
- Waterproof storage
- Multiple locations

## Organization Tips

### Document Binder

Create organized binder:
1. **Section 1:** Identity documents
2. **Section 2:** Financial
3. **Section 3:** Insurance
4. **Section 4:** Medical
5. **Section 5:** Emergency contacts
6. **Section 6:** Property/legal

### Quick Access Card

Wallet-sized card with:
- Emergency contacts
- Medical info
- Insurance policy numbers
- Account numbers

## Annual Review
