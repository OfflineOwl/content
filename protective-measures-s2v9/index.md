# Protective Measures & Basics for CBR Events

> **When Warning Comes** - If you receive warning of a chemical, biological, or radiological event, taking immediate protective measures can significantly reduce exposure.

## Immediate Actions

### When You Hear Warning

1. **Stop what you're doing**
2. **Get inside immediately**
3. **Close all openings**
4. **Turn off air systems**
5. **Monitor emergency broadcasts**

### Time is Critical

- Every minute matters
- Don't gather belongings
- Don't wait for others (they should do the same)
- Move now, communicate later

## Shelter-in-Place

### Quick Setup

1. Go to interior room
2. Close all doors and windows
3. Turn off HVAC
4. Close fireplace damper
5. Seal gaps with tape/wet towels

### What to Bring

- Radio (battery/hand-crank)
- Phone and charger
- Water
- Medications
- First aid kit

## Protection by Threat Type

### Chemical Event

**Priority:** Prevent inhalation

- Get inside above ground level
- Seal room if advised
- Protect breathing (mask, wet cloth)
- Remove contaminated clothing
- Wash exposed skin

### Biological Event

**Priority:** Avoid exposure

- Get inside
- Filter air if possible
- Avoid contact with others
- Monitor for symptoms
- Follow medical guidance

### Radiological Event

**Priority:** Shielding and distance

- Get inside (underground if possible)
- Stay away from windows/walls
- Put mass between you and outside
- Seal room
- Decontaminate if exposed

## Respiratory Protection

### Improvised Protection

If no mask available:
- Multiple layers of wet cloth
- T-shirt over nose/mouth
- Towel wrapped around face
- Any barrier better than none

### Proper Masks

| Mask Type | Protection Level |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Cloth/surgical | Minimal |
| N95 | Good for particles |
| P100 | Better for particles |
| Full respirator | Best (requires fit) |

## Decontamination

### If Exposed Outside

1. Remove outer clothing immediately
2. Bag clothing for disposal
3. Shower thoroughly with soap
4. Wash hair (no conditioner)
5. Put on clean clothes
6. Don't spread contamination

### Don't Contaminate Shelter

- Remove outer clothing before entering
- Have plastic bags ready
- Designate entry/decontamination area
- Shower before joining others

## Monitoring Information

### Sources to Follow

- Emergency Alert System
- Local emergency management
- NOAA Weather Radio
- Official social media
- Local news

### Information to Listen For

- What type of event
- Affected areas
- Duration of shelter
- When to evacuate vs. shelter
- All-clear announcements
