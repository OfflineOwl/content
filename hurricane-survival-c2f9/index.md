# Hurricane & Storm Survival

> **When Storms Approach** - Hurricanes give warning but demand respect. Preparation and proper response during the storm can save lives.

## Hurricane Categories

| Category | Wind Speed | Damage |
|----------|-----------|--------|
| 1 | 74-95 mph | Some damage |
| 2 | 96-110 mph | Extensive damage |
| 3 | 111-129 mph | Devastating damage |
| 4 | 130-156 mph | Catastrophic damage |
| 5 | 157+ mph | Catastrophic damage |

**Categories 3-5 are "major hurricanes."**

## Before the Storm

### Watch vs. Warning

- **Watch:** Conditions possible within 48 hours
- **Warning:** Conditions expected within 36 hours

### Preparation Timeline

**When watch issued (48 hours):**
- Review supplies
- Fill prescriptions
- Gas up vehicles
- Withdraw cash
- Review evacuation routes
- Secure outdoor items

**When warning issued (36 hours):**
- Board windows or install shutters
- Fill bathtub with water
- Charge all devices
- Final supply check
- Make evacuation decision

**24 hours before:**
- Evacuate if ordered or in flood zone
- If staying, move to safe room
- Fill containers with water
- Final preparations complete

### Evacuation Decisions

**Evacuate if:**
- Mandatory evacuation ordered
- In storm surge zone
- In flood zone
- In mobile home (always)
- In high-rise above 10th floor
- Medical needs require it

**Know:**
- Your evacuation zone
- Multiple evacuation routes
- Destination and alternatives
- When to leave (early is better)

## During the Storm

### Shelter in Place

- Stay in interior room away from windows
- Stay on lowest floor that won't flood
- Close all interior doors
- Keep curtains closed
- Stay away from windows and glass doors
- Don't go outside during storm

### The Eye

If the eye passes over:
- Sudden calm, possibly clear sky
- This is temporary (30 min to 2 hours)
- **Do NOT go outside**
- Second half of storm coming
- Winds will return from opposite direction

### Power Outages

- Expect extended outages
- Use flashlights, not candles
- Keep refrigerator closed
- Generator outdoors only
- Unplug electronics (surge protection)

## After the Storm

### Immediate Safety

- Wait for official all-clear
- Avoid downed power lines
- Avoid standing water (contamination, hazards)
- Avoid damaged buildings
- Watch for debris

### Assessment

- Check for injuries
- Check home for damage
- Document damage with photos
- Contact insurance
- Don't enter damaged structures

### Water Safety

After hurricane:
- Assume tap water contaminated until tested
- Use stored water
- Don't wade in flood water
- Watch for displaced wildlife (snakes, etc.)

### Recovery

- Contact FEMA if needed
- Check on neighbors
- Conserve supplies
- Be patient (recovery takes time)
