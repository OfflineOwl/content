# No-Cook Meal Planning

> **When You Can't Use the Stove** - Whether it's a power outage, fuel shortage, or extreme heat, knowing how to eat well without cooking is essential. This guide covers nutritious no-cook meal options.

## Why Plan No-Cook Meals?

You may need no-cook options when:
- Power is out
- Fuel is unavailable
- Fire is dangerous (fire season, indoor safety)
- You need to conserve fuel
- Time is critical
- You're on the move

**Always include no-cook foods in your emergency supplies.**

| Food | Calories | Protein | Shelf Life |

| Canned tuna | 100/can | 22g | 3-5 years |
| Canned chicken | 150/can | 25g | 3-5 years |

| Food | Calories | Notes |
|------|----------|-------|

### Fruits & Vegetables

| Food | Form | Shelf Life |
|------|------|------------|
| Canned fruits | Ready to eat | 2-5 years |
| Dried fruits | Compact, calorie-dense | 1 year |

### Dairy & Alternatives

## No-Cook Meal Ideas

### Breakfast Options

**Cold Cereal Bowl**
- Cereal + shelf-stable milk or powdered milk
- Add dried fruit
- Top with nuts

**Overnight Oats (prepare night before)**
- Oats + water or milk
- Add dried fruit, nuts
- Let sit overnight (no heat needed)

**Peanut Butter Banana**
- Peanut butter on bread or crackers
- Banana or dried fruit
- Trail mix on side

**Granola Parfait**
- Granola + yogurt (shelf-stable)
- Dried fruit
- Honey drizzle

### Lunch Options

**Tuna Salad**
- Canned tuna + mayo packets
- On crackers or bread
- Side of canned vegetables

**Peanut Butter and Jelly**
- Classic comfort food
- High calorie, no cooking
- Works on crackers too

**Bean Salad**
- Canned beans, drained
- Italian dressing packets
- Canned corn or vegetables

**Hummus Wrap**
- Shelf-stable hummus
- Tortilla
- Canned or dried vegetables

### Dinner Options

**Chicken Salad**
- Canned chicken + mayo
- On crackers or bread
- Fruit cup for dessert

**Spam and Crackers**
- Spam slices on crackers
- Canned pineapple
- Vegetable juice

**Beans and Rice (no-cook version)**
- Instant rice (just add water, no heat in warm weather)
- Canned beans
- Hot sauce packets

**Trail Mix Meal**
- Large portion of trail mix
- Jerky
- Dried fruit
- Surprisingly filling

### Snacks

- Trail mix
- Nuts
- Dried fruit
- Crackers with peanut butter
- Protein bars
- Pudding cups
- Applesauce
- Fruit cups

### Day 1

**Breakfast:** Cereal with shelf-stable milk, dried cranberries

## Nutritional Considerations

### Meeting Caloric Needs

**Target:** 2,000 calories per day (more if active)

**No-cook calorie boosters:**
- Peanut butter (190 cal/2 tbsp)
- Nuts (170 cal/oz)
- Trail mix (150 cal/oz)
- Granola (200 cal/cup)
- Dried fruit (100 cal/oz)

### Getting Enough Protein

**Daily need:** 50-60g for adults

**Best no-cook sources:**
- Canned meat (20-25g/can)
- Nuts (5-7g/oz)
- Peanut butter (8g/2 tbsp)
- Beans (8g/half cup)
- Jerky (10g/oz)

### Fiber and Vegetables

Don't neglect these:
- Canned vegetables
- V8 or vegetable juice
- Dried fruit (has fiber)
- Beans
- Whole grain crackers

### Hydration

No-cook eating often means:
- Less moisture from cooking
- Need to drink more water
- Use fruit cups for moisture
- Canned soups (eat cold) provide liquid

## Making No-Cook Foods Palatable

### Condiment Packets

Stock up on:
- Mayonnaise
- Mustard
- Hot sauce
- Soy sauce
- Honey
- Jam/jelly
- Salad dressings
- Salt and pepper

### Seasoning Options

- Pre-mixed seasonings
- Hot sauce
- Dried herbs
- Everything but the Bagel seasoning
- Lemon juice packets

### Presentation Tips

Even in emergencies:
- Use clean plates/bowls when possible
- Combine foods for variety
- Arrange food nicely (morale matters)
- Eat together as a family

## Storage Tips for No-Cook Foods

### Keep Accessible

- Store no-cook items together
- Label "no-cook" box
- Include utensils (sporks, can opener)
- Include condiments

### Rotate Regularly

- Use no-cook items for regular meals
- Check expiration dates
- Replace before they expire
- Try new items before storing large quantities

### Portable Options

For go-bags:
- Focus on lightweight
- Calorie-dense options
- Individual portions
- Long shelf life
- Minimal packaging
