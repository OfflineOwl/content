# Recognizing CBR Warning Signs

> Chemical, biological, and radiological (CBR) incidents are rare. Each one looks different and needs a different first move. Chemical signs show up fast, biological signs can take days, and radiation can't be sensed at all.

## Do This Now

**If you suspect a CHEMICAL release** (strange smell, eye or throat burning, people coughing or collapsing, dead birds or insects, an unexplained cloud or mist):
1. Get away from the area. **Move upwind** and avoid low spots, where heavier-than-air gases collect.
2. Get inside the nearest building, close windows and doors, and turn off ventilation. Shelter in an interior room **above ground**, not the basement.
3. If chemical got on you, take off your clothes (cut off anything that goes over your head), bag them, and wash with soap and water, ideally within 10 minutes.

**If you suspect RADIATION** (an explosion with official warnings of radiation, a nuclear explosion):
1. **Get inside** the nearest sturdy building. Go to the **basement or center**, away from windows and the roof.
2. Take off your outer layer of clothing and wash before joining others.
3. Stay inside at least 24 hours unless officials say otherwise.

**If you see a suspicious powder, spray, or device (possible BIOLOGICAL release):**
1. Get away from it. Don't touch, smell, or clean it up.
2. Cover your mouth and nose with layers of fabric, such as 2-3 layers of cotton, or a mask.
3. Take off and bag your clothes, wash with soap and water, and put on clean clothes.
4. Report it to 911 or local authorities.

**Call 911** (or your local emergency number) if anyone has trouble breathing, seizures, collapse, or burns, and to report a suspected release. **Poison Control: 1-800-222-1222** (US) for questions about chemical exposure.

## Chemical Agent Warning Signs

### How to Recognize

**In the environment:**
- Many dead or dying birds, insects, or small animals
- Unexplained odors (but **some toxic chemicals have no smell**, so no smell doesn't mean safe)
- Oily droplets or films on surfaces
- Low-lying clouds or mist that don't match the weather
- Suspicious spraying

**In people:**
- Difficulty breathing, coughing, chest tightness
- Eyes burning, watering, or blurry vision
- Burning in the nose, throat, or lungs
- Skin irritation, redness, or blisters
- Nausea and vomiting
- Loss of coordination, confusion, twitching, seizures
- **Several people with the same symptoms at once**

### Types of Chemical Agents

| Type | Signs | Examples | Timing |
|------|-------|----------|--------|
| Nerve agents | Pinpoint pupils, drooling, runny nose, heavy sweating, twitching, chest tightness, seizures | Sarin, VX | Seconds to hours |
| Blister agents | Red, itchy, painful skin, then yellow blisters; eye pain and swelling; cough | Sulfur mustard | Often delayed, up to 24 hours |
| Blood agents | Rapid breathing, dizziness, convulsions, collapse | Hydrogen cyanide | Fast |
| Choking agents | Coughing, choking, burning eyes and throat, chest tightness | Chlorine, phosgene | Can be immediate, or delayed with lower exposure |

**Many toxic gases, including chlorine and sulfur mustard, are heavier than air.** They sink and collect in low areas and basements. That's why you move upwind and to higher ground, and shelter above ground.

## Biological Agent Warning Signs

### How to Recognize

There's usually **no immediate sign**. The first clue is often people getting sick days or weeks later.

**Indicators:**
- Many people sick with similar symptoms at the same time
- An unusual disease for the area or season
- Unexplained deaths of animals of the same species
- A suspicious powder, spray device, or letter
- A threat of a biological release

### When to Get Medical Care

Get medical care and tell public health officials if you notice:
- Sudden, severe flu-like illness in otherwise healthy people
- Unusual rashes or skin sores
- Pneumonia-like illness in several people
- Symptoms that match ones officials have described in a news alert

Follow public health instructions about medications or vaccines; don't self-treat with stockpiled antibiotics.

## Radiological Warning Signs

### How to Recognize

**You cannot see, smell, or taste radiation.** Most people will only know from:
- Official alerts and warnings
- A nuclear explosion (blinding flash, blast, mushroom cloud)
- An explosion that officials say involved radioactive material ("dirty bomb")
- Warning signs or labels on a damaged transport container
- Readings from a radiation detector, if you have one

### Signs of Radiation Sickness

Radiation sickness only happens after a large dose in a short time. Early signs can start within minutes to days:
- Nausea and vomiting
- Headache
- Diarrhea

Skin redness or burns may appear within hours or be delayed for days. People often feel better for a while before getting sick again. Get medical care as soon as officials say it's safe to travel.

## Official Warning Systems

### Alert Types

- **Wireless Emergency Alerts (WEA):** alerts on your phone with a special tone and vibration. Free, no sign-up, and not slowed by network congestion. Phones that are off or in airplane mode won't get them.
- **Emergency Alert System (EAS):** interrupts TV and radio broadcasts.
- **NOAA Weather Radio:** 24-hour broadcasts that also carry non-weather emergencies such as chemical releases.
- **Outdoor warning sirens:** meant to warn people who are outside.
- **Local alert sign-ups** from your city or county (text or email).

### Siren Meanings

Siren signals and when they're used differ from community to community, and there's no single national standard. Sirens are meant to be heard outdoors, not inside buildings.

- **When you hear a siren, go inside and get official information** from radio, TV, phone alerts, or NOAA Weather Radio.
- Learn what your local sirens mean from your city or county emergency management office.
- Don't assume it's a test or a weather warning.

## Immediate Response by Situation

### If You're Outdoors

**Chemical:**
- Cover your nose and mouth with cloth and move away quickly, **upwind** and away from low areas
- Don't touch liquids, powders, or anything suspicious
- Get into the nearest building and shelter above ground
- Take off and bag contaminated clothing and wash with soap and water

**Radiation:**
- Get inside the nearest sturdy building immediately. A car is not a shelter.
- Go to the basement or center
- Take off your outer layer of clothing and wash before joining others

**Biological:**
- Get away from the suspicious substance
- Cover your mouth and nose with layers of fabric
- Take off and bag clothing, wash, and report it

### If You're Indoors

- Stay inside unless the release is inside your building (then get out to fresh air)
- Close windows and doors; turn off heating, air conditioning, and fans
- **Chemical:** go to an interior room above ground and seal it with plastic and tape
- **Radiation:** go to the basement or center; no sealing needed
- Monitor emergency broadcasts and follow official instructions

## Quick Reference

```
WARNING SIGNS

CHEMICAL (fast):
- Strange smells (or none at all)
- Dead birds, insects, animals
- Many people sick at once
- Burning eyes, coughing, twitching
-> Move UPWIND, get inside, room ABOVE ground,
   seal it. Clothes off, wash within 10 min.

BIOLOGICAL (slow):
- Many people sick days later
- Unusual illness pattern
- Suspicious powder or spray
-> Get away, cover nose and mouth,
   bag clothes, wash, report.

RADIOLOGICAL (can't sense it):
- Official alerts
- Nuclear flash or blast
-> Get inside, BASEMENT or CENTER,
   outer clothes off, stay 24+ hours.

CALL 911 for breathing trouble, seizures,
collapse, burns, or to report a release.
```
