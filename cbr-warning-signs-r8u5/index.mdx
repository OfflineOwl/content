# Recognizing CBR Warning Signs

> **Chemical, Biological, Radiological Threats** - While rare, CBR events require immediate recognition and response. Knowing the warning signs can save your life.

## Chemical Agent Warning Signs

### How to Recognize

**Environmental signs:**
- Unusual dead/dying animals
- Lack of insect life
- Unexplained odors
- Unusual liquid droplets
- Low-lying clouds or fog unrelated to weather
- Suspicious spraying

**Physical symptoms in people:**
- Difficulty breathing
- Eye irritation/tearing
- Skin irritation/blistering
- Nausea/vomiting
- Convulsions
- Multiple people affected simultaneously

### Types of Chemical Agents

| Type | Effects | Examples |
|------|---------|----------|
| Nerve agents | Muscle twitching, difficulty breathing, seizures | Sarin, VX |
| Blister agents | Skin blisters, eye damage, respiratory damage | Mustard gas |
| Blood agents | Rapid breathing, convulsions | Hydrogen cyanide |
| Choking agents | Coughing, choking, chest tightness | Chlorine, phosgene |

## Biological Agent Warning Signs

### How to Recognize

**Indicators:**
- Unusual illness patterns (many sick at once)
- Unusual disease for region/season
- Sick people with similar symptoms
- Dead animals (same species)
- Unusual spraying/dispersal devices
- Anonymous threats

**Note:** Biological agents often have delayed effects (days to weeks).

### Disease Indicators

Seek medical attention and report if:
- Sudden severe flu-like illness in healthy people
- Unusual rashes or skin lesions
- Pneumonia symptoms in many people
- Multiple deaths from respiratory illness

## Radiological Warning Signs

### How to Recognize

**You cannot see, smell, or taste radiation.**

**Indicators:**
- Explosion with unusual debris
- Official alerts/warnings
- Unusual presence of radioactive materials
- Geiger counter readings (if available)
- Reports of nuclear incident

**Radiation sickness symptoms (delayed):**
- Nausea and vomiting
- Diarrhea
- Skin burns (delayed)
- Weakness
- Hair loss

## Official Warning Systems

### Alert Types

- **Emergency Alert System (EAS):** Radio, TV interruption
- **Wireless Emergency Alerts (WEA):** Cell phone alerts
- **Outdoor warning sirens:** Community notification
- **NOAA Weather Radio:** All-hazards alerts

### Siren Meanings

Varies by location - know your local system:
- Usually a steady tone = take shelter
- Listen to radio for details
- Don't assume you know what it means

## Immediate Response

### If You Suspect CBR Event

1. **Get away from the area**
2. **Move upwind, uphill, upstream**
3. **Get indoors if possible**
4. **Remove contaminated clothing**
5. **Protect breathing (mask, wet cloth)**
6. **Call 911**
7. **Monitor official sources**

### If Outdoors

- Cover mouth and nose
- Move away quickly
- Don't touch anything suspicious
- Get indoors
- Remove and bag outer clothing
- Shower if possible

### If Indoors

- Stay inside
- Close windows and doors
- Turn off HVAC
- Seal gaps
- Move to interior room
- Monitor emergency broadcasts
