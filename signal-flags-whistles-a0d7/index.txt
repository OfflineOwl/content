SIGNAL FLAGS, WHISTLES & VISUAL SIGNALS
=======================================

When electronic communication fails, low-tech signaling methods
can communicate across distances.

WHISTLE SIGNALS
---------------

INTERNATIONAL STANDARDS:

3 BLASTS = DISTRESS/HELP
Universal signal for help needed
Any 3 of anything (whistle, horn, lights)

1 BLAST = ATTENTION
"I'm here" or "listen up"

2 BLASTS = ACKNOWLEDGMENT
"I heard you" / "message received"

FAMILY CODES (CREATE YOUR OWN):
Keep these secret and practice them

Recognition: _______________
All clear: _______________
Come to me: _______________
Danger: _______________

WHISTLE TIPS:
- High-pitched travels farther
- Pea-less whistles work wet
- Attach to yourself (lanyard)
- Blast patterns, not random noise

MIRROR/LIGHT SIGNALS
--------------------

SIGNAL MIRROR:
Reflects sunlight for miles
- Aim at aircraft or search teams
- Flash three times (distress)
- Sweep horizon methodically
- Works during day only

HOW TO USE:
1. Hold mirror near face
2. Look through sighting hole
3. Aim reflection at target
4. Create flash pattern

IMPROVISED MIRROR:
- CD/DVD
- Phone screen
- Any reflective surface
- Metal, glass, foil

FLASHLIGHT SIGNALS:
At night, use flashlight for same patterns:
- 3 flashes = SOS/distress
- Sweep in direction to indicate
- Continuous = "here I am"

SOS IN MORSE CODE:
... --- ... (3 short, 3 long, 3 short)
Works with any signal method

VISUAL GROUND SIGNALS
---------------------

FOR AIRCRAFT (Make LARGE):

X = Need medical help
I = Need supplies
→ = Going this direction
V = Need assistance
N = No (negative)
Y = Yes (affirmative)
F = Need food/water
□ = Need map/compass

SIZE MATTERS:
- Minimum 3 feet wide lines
- 10+ feet ideal
- High contrast to ground
- Use rocks, branches, fabric

PLACEMENT:
- Open areas
- Clear of obstructions
- Visible from above
- Near your location

FLAG SIGNALS
------------

IMPROVISED FLAGS:
Bright clothing works
- Tied to pole/stick
- Waved for attention
- Placed as marker

DISTRESS FLAG:
Orange/bright color
Wave actively for attention
Not at half-staff (indicates death)

SIGNALING WITH FLAG:
Wave side to side = attention
Hold still = ready for message
Move in circles = SOS

COLORED FLAGS/CLOTH:
Red = danger or stop
Orange = distress
Green = safe or go
White = truce or surrender

SMOKE SIGNALS
-------------

SMOKE DURING DAY:
Visible from great distances
- Green vegetation = white smoke
- Rubber/plastic = black smoke (toxic)
- Create contrast with sky

THREE FIRES:
International distress signal
Arranged in triangle
Must be maintained

FLARE SIGNALS
-------------

TYPES:
- Pencil flares (small)
- Handheld flares (road type)
- Aerial flares (parachute)
- Smoke flares (daytime)

USE:
- Save for when rescue is near
- Three flares = distress
- Read instructions first
- Keep dry

SAFETY:
- Burn hazard
- Fire hazard
- Point away from self
- Don't look at flame

BODY SIGNALS
------------

FOR AIRCRAFT:

NEED HELP:
Both arms raised in Y shape

YES/AFFIRMATIVE:
One arm raised

NO/NEGATIVE:
Arms crossed or waved horizontally

GO THIS WAY:
Point with both arms

STAY THERE:
Arms straight out to sides

LAND HERE:
Squat, arms forward pointing to ground
