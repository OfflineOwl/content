🏠 Bug-In vs Bug-Out Decision Guide

An interactive tool to help you make the right choice in an emergency

The Critical Question: When disaster strikes, should you shelter in place (bug-in) or evacuate (bug-out)? The wrong choice can be dangerous. Use this tool to assess your situation.

📋 Situation Assessment

Answer these questions based on your current situation. Be honest—your safety depends on it.

1 Is your home structurally safe right now?
2 What are authorities recommending?
3 Do you have adequate supplies at home?
4 Is the threat ongoing or escalating?
5 Do you have a safe evacuation route?
6 Do you have a planned destination?
7 Family mobility considerations?
🏠

Recommendation: BUG-IN (Shelter in Place)

Confidence Score

Based on your answers, staying home is likely the safer option. Your home provides shelter, you have supplies, and evacuation presents more risk than benefit.

  • Secure your home and seal if necessary
  • Gather family in safest room
  • Monitor emergency broadcasts
  • Conserve resources
  • Be ready to evacuate if situation changes
🚗

Recommendation: BUG-OUT (Evacuate)

Confidence Score

Based on your answers, leaving is likely the safer option. Staying presents significant risks that outweigh the challenges of evacuation.

  • Grab your go-bag and essential documents
  • Follow your planned evacuation route
  • Notify your out-of-area contact
  • Turn off utilities if time allows
  • Don't return until authorities say it's safe

🏠 Bug-In When...

  • Home is structurally safe
  • You have adequate supplies
  • Roads are dangerous/blocked
  • No evacuation order issued
  • Threat is external (civil unrest)
  • Weather makes travel dangerous
  • You have mobility limitations
  • No safe destination available

🚗 Bug-Out When...

  • Mandatory evacuation ordered
  • Home is damaged/unsafe
  • Threat is approaching (fire, flood)
  • Chemical/radiation hazard nearby
  • Violence in your area
  • Utilities failed long-term
  • Safe route and destination exist
  • Staying = certain danger

⚠️ Override Conditions

Regardless of other factors, evacuate immediately if:

  • You smell gas or see fire approaching
  • Floodwaters are rising to your level
  • Structure is collapsing or severely damaged
  • Active violence/threat at your location
  • Authorities order mandatory evacuation
