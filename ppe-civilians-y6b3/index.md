# PPE Basics for Civilians

> **Personal Protection Equipment** - Knowing when and how to use basic protective equipment can significantly reduce your exposure to hazards during emergencies.

## Respiratory Protection

### Types of Masks

| Type | Protects Against | Fit |
|------|------------------|-----|
| Cloth mask | Large droplets | Loose |
| Surgical mask | Droplets, some particles | Loose |
| N95 | 95% of particles ≥0.3μm | Tight |
| KN95 | Similar to N95 | Tight |
| P100 | 99.97% of particles | Tight |

### When to Use Masks

- Airborne disease exposure
- Smoke and poor air quality
- Dust and debris
- Mold cleanup
- Chemical exposure (appropriate rating)

### Proper Mask Use

1. Wash hands before touching mask
2. Cover nose AND mouth
3. Ensure seal around face (fit check)
4. Don't touch front while wearing
5. Replace when damp or damaged
6. Wash hands after removal

## Eye Protection

### Types

- **Safety glasses:** Basic impact protection
- **Goggles:** Splash and particle protection
- **Face shield:** Face and eye protection

### When to Use

- Cleanup work (debris)
- Chemical exposure
- Infectious disease (close contact)
- Smoke and irritants
- Power tool use

## Hand Protection

### Glove Types

| Material | Use For |
|----------|---------|
| Nitrile | Medical, chemicals, general |
| Latex | Medical (allergy concerns) |
| Vinyl | Light duty, food |
| Leather | Physical work |
| Rubber | Cleaning, chemicals |

### Proper Glove Use

1. Check for holes before use
2. Wash hands before putting on
3. Don't touch face while wearing
4. Remove without touching outside
5. Wash hands after removal
6. Single use = dispose properly

### Removal Technique

1. Pinch outside of one glove
2. Peel off, holding in gloved hand
3. Slide finger under remaining glove
4. Peel off over first glove
5. Dispose properly
6. Wash hands

## Body Protection

### Work Clothing

- Long pants and sleeves
- Sturdy closed-toe shoes
- High visibility (if needed)
- Weather appropriate

### Protective Suits

**Tyvek suits:**
- Dust and particles
- Light chemical splash
- Cleanup work

**Full protective suits:**
- Serious chemical exposure
- Requires training

## Head Protection

### Hard Hats

**Use for:**
- Falling debris
- Construction areas
- Low clearance
- Cleanup work

## Hearing Protection

### Types

- Foam earplugs
- Earmuffs
- Electronic ear protection

### When to Use

- Power tool operation
- Generator operation
- Loud environments
- Extended noise exposure
