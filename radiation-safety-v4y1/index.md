# Radiation Safety Fundamentals

> **Understanding the Invisible Threat** - Radiation cannot be seen, smelled, or tasted, but understanding basic principles can help you protect yourself during a radiological emergency.

## Key Principles

### Time, Distance, Shielding

**TIME:** Minimize exposure time
- Less time = less dose
- Get away quickly
- Don't linger near sources

**DISTANCE:** Increase distance from source
- Radiation decreases rapidly with distance
- Double the distance = 1/4 the exposure
- Move away as quickly as possible

**SHIELDING:** Put material between you and source
- Heavier materials provide better shielding
- Concrete, brick, earth most effective
- Even walls provide some protection

## Types of Radiation Events

| Event | Threat | Response |
|-------|--------|----------|
| Nuclear power plant | Radioactive release | Shelter or evacuate |
| Dirty bomb | Conventional + radiological | Shelter, decontaminate |
| Nuclear weapon | Blast + radiation + fallout | Shelter in place |

## Shelter-in-Place

### When to Shelter

- Official instruction given
- Nuclear event reported
- Unable to safely evacuate

### Where to Shelter

**Best protection:**
- Underground (basement)
- Center of large building
- Middle floors of tall buildings

**Protection factors:**
- Wood house: 2x
- Brick/concrete: 10x
- Underground: 100x+

### How Long

- Initial fallout most dangerous in first hours
- Stay sheltered for at least 24 hours if possible
- Listen for official guidance
- Longer shelter = less exposure

## Decontamination Basics

### If Exposed to Fallout

1. Remove outer clothing (removes ~90% of contamination)
2. Bag and set aside clothing
3. Shower or wash with soap and water
4. Don't scrub skin roughly
5. Wash hair with shampoo (no conditioner)
6. Blow nose, wipe ears, wipe eyelids
7. Put on clean clothes

### What NOT to Do

- Don't use conditioner (binds particles)
- Don't scrub skin (opens pores)
- Don't spread contamination
- Don't panic

## Potassium Iodide (KI)

### What It Does

- Protects thyroid from radioactive iodine
- Does NOT protect from other radiation types
- Most effective if taken before exposure

### Who Should Take It

- Per official guidance only
- Most beneficial for young people
- May be distributed during nuclear emergency

### Limitations

- Only protects thyroid
- Only effective for radioactive iodine
- Not a radiation cure-all
